## Potential Applications

### Supervisory Control Layer
Monitor autonomous agent state vectors to detect early signs of "rigidity" (overfitting to a local optimum). An agent whose Coherence climbs above 95 while Adaptability drops below 20 is exhibiting the Stage 8 Rigidity Risk (Illusion of Permanence) pattern — the classic value lock-in signature.

### Constitutional AI Tuning
Use the 42 Negative Confessions (Ma'at declarations) as a lightweight ethical constraint checker. Each confession maps to a behavioral boundary. Violations register as NSDT tension spikes that the engine surfaces as trap states.

### Decision Pressure Monitoring
The KAIROS Decision Signal (ACT / PARADOX / DELAY / REFLECT) provides a human-interpretable readout of agent certainty and conflict state. High pressure with low confidence maps directly to DELAY — the appropriate response for an AI system operating near the boundary of its training distribution.

### Simulation of Human-AI Interaction
Model cognitive load and decision pressure in human operators overseeing AI systems. A human operator at Stage 8 (Illusion of Permanence) is statistically more likely to over-trust AI output. KAIROS flags this state proactively.

## The Cultural Intelligence Layer

- Code-switching (adaptive cognitive flexibility misread as instability)
- Historical trauma load (intergenerational burden misread as individual deficit)
- Systemic oppression (externally imposed performance suppression misread as capability ceiling)
